About us · เกี่ยวกับร้าน

Udon Thani's som tam, the way locals like it.

Somtam Je Kai has been feeding Udon Thani for more than 20 years. Branch 2 sits on Naresuan Road near the Wat Ban Chik intersection. It's a busy, friendly local shop where every plate is pounded fresh in the mortar, spicy the way you ask for it.

Come in for a quick lunch, a family dinner or a big table with friends. We have an air-conditioned dining room with seating for more than 60, plus tables out front.

Menu · เมนูเด็ด

Pounded fresh, spicy as you like

A taste of what comes out of our kitchen. The full menu is on every table, so tick what you want and we'll start pounding.

Classic

Som tam ส้มตำ

Green papaya, chilli, lime and fish sauce, pounded to order. Thai-style, with salted egg, or the full mixed platter.

In season

Santol salad ตำกระท้อน

Sour-sweet santol fruit pounded tam-style. Ask if it's on today. We serve it while santol is in season.

From the grill

Grilled fish ปลาเผา

Whole fish crusted in salt and slow-grilled. Perfect alongside your som tam.

Corn som tam ตำข้าวโพด

Sweet corn kernels, tomato and carrot pounded in our tam dressing. A favourite with kids and anyone who wants it milder.

Seafood plates อาหารทะเลรสแซ่บ

Squid and shrimp tossed with chilli, lime and fresh vegetables. Big, bright and made for sharing.

Khanom jeen ขนมจีน

Soft fresh rice noodles to eat with your tam and soak up all that dressing.
